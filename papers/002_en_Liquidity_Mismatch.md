## 1. Core Link: Liquidity Structural Imbalance and Five-Fold Idleness

Monetary liquidity — its aggregate looseness or tightness, and its structural allocation — directly shapes the allocation of the five idle factors.

A global phenomenon cuts across all three economies: **money is abundant in aggregate, yet structurally blocked on its way to the real economy** — sectors that need capital cannot get it, while others drown in over-investment.

- **China**: real-estate SOEs borrow at 1.75%, while small manufacturers pay 7%+ (a near 4× gap).
- **United States**: post-pandemic easing inflated asset prices, while capital favored financial assets and tech giants over manufacturing; "Rust Belt" capacity starved of upgrading finance.
- **EU**: a single monetary policy cannot fit 20 divergent members — southern economies need stimulus, northern ones restraint; one rate guarantees structural mismatch.

The financial system's fatal bias: **it favors "collateralizable assets" (land, property, machines) and rejects "non-collateralizable capacity" (labor, skill, time)** — which is why the *time* dimension of idleness is entirely invisible to finance.

## 2. Industry: The Credit Expansion-Contraction Trap

### 2.1 China — Photovoltaic overcapacity

2010–2012: post-stimulus credit loosening drove PV module capacity from ~30GW to ~80GW against ~40GW global demand, utilization collapsing below 50%. [Simulated — historical review; figures per industry association] The later credit tightening (2018–2020) left firms unable to upgrade, freezing old capacity into structural idleness.

### 2.2 United States — "Financialization squeeze"

Manufacturing utilization 75.4% (Fed, 2024). Capital prefers high-return financial assets over long-cycle manufacturing; the 2023 Silicon Valley Bank failure exposed liquidity circulating within finance while real-economy financing tightened.

### 2.3 EU — Divergence under one rate

A single euro-area rate cannot simultaneously suit Germany (restraint) and Southern Europe (stimulus), leaving southern industrial capacity chronically idle.

## 3. Land and Resources: Liquidity Hollows and Asset Idleness

- **Land**: Chinese county-level agricultural borrowers face 6–8% effective costs with sub-40% approval; U.S. farmland and Rust Belt land suffer collateral rejection; Southern European depopulated regions freeze without development finance.
- **Resources**: U.S. office vacancy (18.4%, Yardi) follows a liquidity cliff — remote work shrinks rent cash flow, collapses mortgage value, locks refinancing, and deepens vacancy: a vicious "vacant → devalued → harder to finance → more vacant" spiral.

---

## 4. Labor and Time: Liquidity Stratification

- **China**: 110 million hidden unemployed behind SME financing difficulty (sub-25% approval in hospitality/retail).
- **United States**: participation 62.5% (BLS) partly reflects those exiting the labor force without re-entry support.
- **EU**: ~3 million idle youth tied to credit-tight southern firms unwilling to hire.

**Time is the "shadow form" of liquidity mismatch**: every jobless person the mismatch creates is simultaneously a "time-idle" person — whose leisure is passively consumed because finance refuses to recognize non-collateralizable labor and time.

---

## 5. Three Blockages in Liquidity Transmission

1. **Collateral bias**: finance recognizes assets, not ability.
2. **Sector labeling**: one-size-fits-all credit classification cannot distinguish cyclical from structural surplus.
3. **Information asymmetry**: small actors' true needs and risks go unread.

---

## 6. Conclusion

The financial root of five-fold idleness, in one sentence:

> **A financial system that takes money as the only measure of credit will always favor collateralizable assets and ignore non-collateralizable labor and time.**

Breaking the five-fold idleness therefore requires more than liquidity easing — it needs an **exchange and credit mechanism that does not take money as the only measure**, letting "labor" and "time" enter the credit system. This relies on a technical precondition: **distributed trustworthy credentialing** that provides tamper-proof, traceable exchange records.
