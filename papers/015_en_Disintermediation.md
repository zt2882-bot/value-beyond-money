## 1. The Value-Dilution Mechanism

Intermediaries and e-commerce platforms are **value-extraction layers** in the production-consumption chain: they create no value, yet dilute the value between producer and consumer through channels, commissions, and traffic allocation.

- **Traditional intermediaries**: layer upon layer of markup, hurting both ends;
- **E-commerce platforms**: commissions + traffic bidding, further compressing producer profit.

The shared phenomenon across economies: **producers "produce more but earn less," consumers "buy more but gain less," while the middle takes the lion's share** — the circulation-side expression of the distributional imbalance (Paper 4).

## 5. Conclusion

Disintermediation is the **"liberation path of producer's usable value."** It returns the value diluted by the middle layer to producers and consumers — freeing value space for the "capacity" and "resources" dimensions of five-fold idleness.
