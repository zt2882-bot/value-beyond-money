# Paper 20: Promotion Paths for the Open Matching Exchange Platform

**Data principle: real data is sourced; projections are always marked "Simulated."**

---

## 1. Promotion Basis

[Simulated] Pilots in county agriculture and manufacturing alliances have verified feasibility; promotion is not "one-size-fits-all" but "region-by-region, phase-by-phase" graded rollout. — Projected.

Fit conditions: communities and regions with **idle resources, willingness to cooperate, and basic digital literacy.**

---

## 2. The Path: Regional, Phased Landing

1. **Pilot**: county agriculture, manufacturing alliances, community time banks — small-scale verification;
2. **Replication**: horizontal replication to similar communities/regions, forming regional networks;
3. **Interconnection**: regional networks interconnect, forming cross-regional exchange-matching networks;
4. **Ecosystem**: connect with finance, welfare, and public services, forming a complete ecosystem.

---

## 3. Long-Term Evolution

- **Technology**: from "recording" to "intelligent matching + automatic settlement";
- **Function**: from "exchange matching" to "credit, security, finance";
- **Ecosystem**: from "de-commercialized exchange platform" to "social infrastructure."

---

## 4. The Principles to Guard

The platform's biggest risk is **re-commercialization after success** — re-capture by capital, renewed commissions, renewed data monopoly. Three principles must be guarded:

1. **Open-source**: code and rules forever open;
2. **Non-profit**: never commission/monopoly as the profit model;
3. **Data autonomy**: user data always belongs to the user.

**These three principles are the moat of the platform's de-commercialization — the moment they fall, the platform degrades into "another commercial platform."**

---

## 5. Conclusion

The platform's promotion and evolution is the **"growth vein of a de-capital-dependent economic ecosystem."** Its success depends not on technology, but on **never betraying the "open-source, non-profit, data-autonomous" original intent in the course of scaling.**
