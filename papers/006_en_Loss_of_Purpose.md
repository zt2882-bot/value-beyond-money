# Paper 6: Loss of Purpose in Production and Consumption

**Data principle: real data is sourced; projections are always marked "Simulated."**

---

## 1. The Concept

Another root of five-fold idleness is the **purpose-fracture between production and consumption**:

- **Production** no longer aims at "satisfying real needs" but at "appreciating under the money measure" — producing for profit, hence surplus;
- **Consumption** no longer aims at "real need" but at "identity, stimulation, comparison" — consuming for symbols, hence waste.

Across all three economies, **blind production and blind consumption coexist, separated by money — the sole medium — which carries no information about "what is needed."**

---

## 2. The Reality of Failed Matching

- **China**: overcapacity (steel, cement) coexists with under-supply of real needs (eldercare, childcare, primary healthcare);
- **United States**: commercial real estate surplus (18.4% office vacancy, Yardi) coexists with affordable-housing shortage — "wrong buildings" and "missing homes" at once;
- **EU**: southern youth unemployment (Spain 25%) coexists with northern skill shortages — "idle people" and "vacant posts" that cannot match.

The shared fact: **it is not "no supply" or "no demand," but "no channel that transmits real purpose."**

---

## 3. The Roots

1. **Money's information poverty**: money carries only "price," not "purpose, need, preference" — so purpose is erased in monetization;
2. **Capital's logic**: capital produces for appreciation, preferring quick-monetizable products over real needs;
3. **Consumption's symbolism**: advertising and status anxiety manufacture "non-real needs."

**Precise matching is fundamentally impossible inside a pure money framework — because money was never designed for precise matching.**

---

## 4. The Path: A Purpose-Oriented Matching System

The direction is to build, outside money, a channel where **"purpose" can be expressed and matched**:

- **Demand side**: express "what I need and why" (not just "what I'll pay");
- **Supply side**: make "what I offer and its real use" identifiable (not just "price");
- **Matching side**: match by purpose, not price.

This is the logical starting point of moneyless exchange (Paper 9) and the open matching platform (Paper 19).

---

## 5. Conclusion

The root of production waste is not "producing too much," but "purpose misaligned between production and demand." The cure is not "producing less," but **"aligning production with real purpose"** — which requires an exchange mechanism that transmits purpose.
