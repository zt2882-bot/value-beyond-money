# Paper 10: Cross-Category Exchange Under a Retained Price Scale

**Data principle: real data is sourced; projections are always marked "Simulated."**

---

## 1. Definition

Paper 9's moneyless exchange abandons price entirely. But some exchanges need a **value reference** to anchor equivalence — hence "price-scale exchange":

> **Keep "price" as the reference ruler, but settle not in money — barter directly across categories.**

| Mode | Value measure | Settlement |
|---|---|---|
| Monetary | price | money |
| Moneyless | labor time | product/service |
| **Price-scale** | **price (reference only)** | **direct cross-category swap** |

Its common ground with moneyless exchange: **neither settles in money.**

---

## 2. Theoretical Basis

Price (ideally) approximates "socially necessary labor time." Retaining the price scale is therefore **keeping the reference for value equivalence while stripping away the monetary settlement** — letting two "equivalent" products swap directly, skipping money's friction (exchange, settlement, arbitrage).

It avoids moneyless exchange's equivalence problem (no reference) and monetary exchange's intermediary friction.

---

## 3. Mechanism

1. **Price anchoring**: fix an "equivalence ratio" by market price (e.g., 1 refrigerator ≈ 200kg steel);
2. **Direct swap**: swap at the ratio, no money;
3. **Third-party inspection**: ensure quality and fairness;
4. **Credit credentialing**: swap records on an immutable ledger.

---

## 4. Practice and Effects

[Simulated] A 30-enterprise platform: swap surplus refrigerators for steel, idle lines for components; inventory down 32%, ~40 million yuan in procurement saved. — Projected.

Real reference: supply-chain barter has long existed, but its scale was limited by "hard-to-pin equivalence" — which **big-data matching + trustworthy credentialing** now fill.

---

## 5. Conditions and Challenges

- **Safeguards**: authoritative price references (against manipulated ratios), neutral inspection, trustworthy credentialing;
- **Fit**: standardized, clearly-priced products (commodities, industrial goods); not unpriced services and labor;
- **Optimization**: trustworthy credentialing for equivalence disputes, alliance organization for fulfillment.

---

## 6. Conclusion

Price-scale exchange is the **bridge between money and moneyless exchange**. It does not abolish price, but strips the monetary settlement, letting "equivalent products" circulate directly. For five-fold idleness, it is the most direct channel for the "capacity" and "resources" dimensions — especially for surplus products that are priced and standardized.
