# Paper 12: Releasing Monetary Anxiety and Its Effect on Productive Power

**Data principle: real data is sourced; projections are always marked "Simulated."**

---

## 1. The Concept

"Monetary anxiety" is the pervasive insecurity arising from **dependence on money alone** — income uncertain, future uncertain, only hoarding money feels safe.

It is the **psychological amplifier** of five-fold idleness:

- More anxiety → less consumption → weaker demand → more capacity idleness;
- More anxiety → less risk-taking (starting, switching, learning) → more labor and time idleness.

The same anxiety runs through all three economies: China's "precautionary saving," America's middle-class stagnation anxiety, Europe's youth uncertainty.

---

## 2. The Root: Dependence on Money Alone

The root is not "too little money" but **"no second asset besides money that brings security."**

In a money-only society, time, skill, and labor capacity are all "non-redeemable" — so people hoard money and cannot convert "time and labor" into security.

**Time Banking is the counterexample**: when "one hour of labor" is storable and spendable, the individual gains a "second security beyond money" — and anxiety eases.

---

## 3. Paths to Release

1. **Time saving**: store and redeem labor time (time banking);
2. **Skill sharing**: participate in moneyless exchange with skill as an asset;
3. **Idle-resource swap**: join price-scale exchange with idle capacity and space.

Their common effect: **extend "money-only" security into "money + time + skill + idle resources" security.**

---

## 4. Projected Effects

[Simulated] Releasing monetary anxiety projects into: repaired expectations (saving falls, consumption and investment rise), activated idleness (the unemployed's time becomes exchangeable), and rebuilt social connection.

---

## 5. Conclusion

Releasing monetary anxiety is the **psychological-empowerment engine of high-quality development**. It relies not on handing out money but on **giving individuals "value confirmation beyond money"** — making time and labor "redeemable security" again.
