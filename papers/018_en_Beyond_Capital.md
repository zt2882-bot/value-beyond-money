# Paper 18: Alliances Beyond Capital Control

**Data principle: real data is sourced; projections are always marked "Simulated."**

---

## 1. Deconstructing Capital's Grip

Capital's control over producers concentrates in three links: **capital (financing), channel (sales), and data (user and transaction information).** For a producer alliance to be truly autonomous, it must **break free of capital control in all three.**

---

## 2. The Shift from Dependence to Autonomy

| Link | Capital's grip | Alliance's alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Capital | financing dependence (interest, equity) | internal mutual-aid + capacity swap |
| Channel | platform commission, traffic monopoly | self-built channels (Paper 15) |
| Data | user & transaction data owned by platform | self-held data, data autonomy |

**The key shift: from "passive dependence on capital" to "autonomous control of the three links."**

---

## 3. Cases

Real references: open-source software communities (data autonomy, decentralization, non-profit); cooperative banks (internal financing, reduced dependence on commercial capital).

[Simulated] An alliance cutting external dependence ~50% through internal financing + self-built channels + self-held data. — Projected.

---

## 4. Challenges and Optimization

- **Capital's counterattack**: channel blockade, predatory pricing, data walls — countered by open platforms (Paper 19);
- **Autonomy capability**: technology, management, credit — lowered by trustworthy credentialing.

---

## 5. Conclusion

The alliance beyond capital control is the **"grassroots carrier of economic fairness and autonomy."** It moves producers from "passive dependence on capital" to "autonomous control" — the organizational precondition for the autonomous absorption of five-fold idleness (especially capacity, resources, and data).
